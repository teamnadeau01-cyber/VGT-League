League Rules

1. Sportsmanship

Players must treat opponents, staff and community members with respect.

2. Fair Play

Cheating, exploiting or intentionally abusing bugs is not allowed.

3. Match Conduct

Teams must follow official match procedures and listen to referees.

4. Staff Decisions

League staff and referees have the final say during official matches.

5. Roster Rules

Players must be registered on their team's official roster before competing.

6. Penalties

Breaking league rules may result in warnings, match penalties, suspensions or removal.
